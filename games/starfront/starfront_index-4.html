STARFRONT : FRONTIER

A top-down space survival shooter. Mine, fight, evolve, and build your fleet.

Start with a nimble Scout. Collect glowing crystals from asteroids, defeat raiders, earn XP, and use the base to repair, improve your stats, and unlock a 20-ship evolution tree.

Desktop: WASD/arrow keys to move, mouse to aim, click or Space to fire, Shift to boost, E to dock. Mobile: use the directional and fire buttons.

Progress saves automatically on this device.

Unlock the next ship in your evolution line. Ships require the previous ship in their branch. Your owned ships stay unlocked; choose any owned ship to pilot.

Permanent upgrades apply to your current ship. Upgrade hull, weapon damage, engine speed, fire rate, energy and mining yield.

Strengthen your home base so you have a safer place to dock and recover.
